Generated by `simulate_intervention.py`. Re-run after editing `ASSUMPTIONS` in that script.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `before_after_dropoff.png` | Main chart: weekly share active for would-be withdrawers (Jordan) and would-be fails (Sam) reached by at least one intervention, before vs after |

## How it works
- **Baseline.** studentVle.csv is not in the dataset folder, so the weekly share-active curves are transcribed from `engagement_by_week.png` (the earlier full-OULAD analysis). The simulated "before" matches them within 1.6 points every week. Group sizes come from studentInfo.csv; assessment weeks (3, 7, 14, 18, 23, 28) from assessments.csv.
- **Model.** Share active = (share still engaged) x (chance an engaged learner is active that week). Interventions cut the weekly dropout hazard and nudge in-week activity, only inside their windows and only for learners they reach. Each learner keeps the same random draws, so "after" is the same learner with the intervention.

## Assumptions (edit in the script)
| Intervention | Window | Reach | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| C2 One small thing | weeks 0-3 | 60% of withdrawers, 50% of fails | dropout hazard -40%, activity +10% |
| C6 Deadline runway | 2 weeks before each TMA | 40% / 50% | hazard -25%, activity +8% |
| C7 Welcome back | weeks 10-13, 24-27 | 40% / 50%, 30% of pass/distinction | hazard -35%, recovers 30% of the break dip |

## Caveats
- Effect sizes are assumptions, not estimates. Change them and the chart changes.
- Weekly activity is drawn independently within the engaged spell, so the simulated median last active week for fails is 16, not the real 14 (withdrawers match at 7).
- The pass-rate projection in `simulated_summary.csv` assumes an extra active week = 2 active days, at ~2 points per day (from the earlier analysis).
